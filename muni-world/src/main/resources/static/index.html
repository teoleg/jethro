Load an Official Statement PDF (deep terms for one issue)
Which PDF? Only the one titled “Official Statement” (the final OS for that series) — it holds the call schedule, original issue prices and tax status. Skip annual reports, ACFRs, event notices and amendments; they carry none of that. The fastest route: select a bond below → click “open this CUSIP on EMMA” in its detail panel → EMMA’s page for that exact security links its OS → download it.

1. Put the OS PDF in os-inbox/ (also scanned on a schedule).  2. Click Load. The parser reads the maturity schedule and lands EVERY CUSIP in that series — one OS typically covers dozens of your bonds.
